ISLAHE NAFS

by Dr. Mohsin Ali

A person does not have to strive too hard to be evil. If he does not try to become good then he becomes evil by default. It’s similar to situation that you can grow good crop if you put in effort but the same field can become a habitat of insects and reptiles and it would have thorny bushes. The good initiative and efforts of Dawah can lead to a good life and help one to become a better human being.
